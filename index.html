Narrative Planner

AI-guided interviews that capture a lifetime of stories

Narrative Planner is an intelligent interview system that combines a GraphRAG knowledge graph with a coverage-aware planning agent to conduct deep, structured memoir interviews. It tracks which parts of a person's life have been explored and strategically guides each question toward uncovered territory — ensuring no chapter goes untold.

Live Demo GitHub

What is Narrative Planner?

Capturing a person's complete life story in an interview is surprisingly difficult. Conversations naturally gravitate toward vivid, emotionally salient memories while quieter decades — careers, friendships, migrations, everyday rituals — go unmentioned. Existing AI interviewers lack any mechanism to track coverage or detect these gaps.

Narrative Planner addresses this with a two-layer architecture: a GraphRAG module that builds a structured life-event knowledge graph in real time as the interview unfolds, and an intelligent planning agent that reads coverage signals from the graph to decide whether to explore the current topic more deeply or pivot to an underrepresented area of the subject's life.

The result is richer, more complete memoirs. Across automated evaluations using simulated interviewee personas, Narrative Planner achieves substantially higher life-coverage scores than a strong baseline chatbot interviewer — without sacrificing the conversational warmth that makes these sessions feel human.

32% improvement in life coverage score vs. baseline

How It Works

Interview

A conversational AI engages the subject in natural dialogue, asking open-ended questions guided by a coverage-aware planning agent that tracks what has and hasn't been discussed.

Graph Construction

As the conversation flows, life events, people, places, and time periods are extracted and woven into a real-time GraphRAG knowledge graph that maps the subject's life.

Coverage Navigation

The planner continuously evaluates graph coverage, balancing rich exploration of well-remembered topics with strategic pivots toward underrepresented decades and themes.
